## Implemented boundary

### W1-X2

- The P1 fake accepts only externally committed text, keeps partial recognition at zero synthesis effects, validates exact interaction/response identity, and reports `demo_substitute` rather than `formal`.
- The P2 fake proves committed-turn submission returns while a controlled Agent remains delayed. Response replacement fences late Agent output from UI, history, and audio effects.
- The P3alpha fake preserves exact task and attempt identity from committed task intent through TaskEvent-derived WorkProgress. The Bridge never calls TTS and does not own Task state.
- Each track can be marked unavailable independently. An unavailable track reports `unsupported`; one track's failure does not change either of the other tracks.

### W1-P1B

- Browser Speech recognition observations carry exact capture generation, sequence, raw/display text, unknown confidence, fallback provenance, and an explicit `commits_turn=false` fact. Browser provider-final chunks still pass through the existing logical capture and commit gate.
- Browser Speech synthesis preserves exact local response generation and fences stale/wrong-response callbacks. It exposes batch compatibility only; formal recognition streaming, hypothesis cursor, synthesis streaming, and audio chunk cursor remain unsupported.
- The opt-in `VITE_FEATURE_LIVE_VOICE_INTEGRATED_P1` route selects the compatibility player and observation mapper without moving conversation or commit authority into `useLiveVoiceDemo`.
- Route telemetry is emitted only when recognition is actually started, synthesis is actually queued, or an unavailable capability is actually attempted. It identifies the Adapter owner and reports `fallback` or `unsupported` truthfully.
- With the flag disabled, the existing Browser player and recognition callback path remain selected. The new route creates no timer, listener, network request, store entry, or task side effect.
