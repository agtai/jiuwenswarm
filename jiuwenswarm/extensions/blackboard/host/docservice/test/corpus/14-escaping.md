# Escaping

Literal asterisks: \*not italic\* and \*\*not bold\*\*.

Literal underscores: \_not italic\_ and snake_case_name.

\# Not a heading at the start of the line.

1\. Not a list item.

\- Not a bullet either.

Backslashes: C:\\Users\\alice and a single \\ backslash.

Angle brackets and ampersands: a < b, c > d, Tom & Jerry, &copy; entity.

Brackets: \[not a link\](nope) and a lone ] bracket.

Backticks in text: use \`npm\` literally.
